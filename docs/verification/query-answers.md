**What is operational memory, and does the evidence show it improves performance?**

The working definition is a reusable record preserving a decision, its rationale
and evidence so later readers can inspect, apply or challenge it. The
[concept note](v2-compiled-vault/wiki/concepts/operational-memory.md) links the
protocol, research memo and EPUB that support this definition. Those sources do not
measure benefits. The [CSV comparison](v2-compiled-vault/wiki/comparisons/metrics-observations.md)
contains an unexplained 85%/90% revision difference and cannot establish causation,
statistical significance or real organizational performance.
